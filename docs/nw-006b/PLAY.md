No development harness, query parameter, console command or test shortcut is needed. The station environment is included in this branch; no separate asset install is required.

Controls: mouse look, WASD movement, Shift sprint, Ctrl/C crouch, E interaction, Escape pause.

Watch her walk through the east gap and check the station. You keep full movement/look control. This introduction and her initial repositioning are safe; sprinting, taking your time or looking away will not cause an early death.

Try crouching (Ctrl/C) behind the linen trolley. Its covered side blocks sight from most of the station, but its east end is open.105's bed and the station's staff locker are real hiding places: use the contextual interaction key to enter, then use it again to leave when the exit is clear. Walk quietly, use broken sightlines, avoid entering concealment in front of her and watch the route before crossing. The first alert leaves time to recover; it is not an instant capture.

Reach the corridor exit east of the station, beyond the treatment doorway. Completion is a checkpoint, with no intrusive completion screen. Explore onward without a forced later chase. Escape pauses/settings; Resume returns to the same phase. Restart/Continue restore the latest encounter milestone. New Game clears the opening and encounter progression.
