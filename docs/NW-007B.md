# NW-007B — Nurse Shaw cinematic animation and atmosphere

**Status: draft for review. Do not merge.** This is the presentation half of NW-007, under the NW-007
Shared Design Contract. It contains no gameplay director, trigger, checkpoint or UI logic; those
belong to Codex (NW-007A). It does not attempt the full character overhaul planned for NW-008.

- **Baseline:** `0097264` (PR #21, `feat/nw-006b-nurse-stealth`), verified, branched exactly.
- **Branch:** `claude/nw-007b-cinematic-assets`.

## 2. Nurse Shaw (Phase 2)

### 2.1 Animations
New and reworked clips live in **`src/horror/entities/nurseClips.ts`**. That one module is used by
the Blender bake (`samplePoses.test.ts` → `rig_and_export.py`), by the runtime fallback, and by this
contract. Previews of each clip (Blender, from the exported game rig) are in
`docs/nw-007b/clips/*.gif`. Key frames from the side and front are in
`docs/nw-007b/audit/clips-side-front.jpg`.

- **Idle and walk** keep their names, loop flags and gait numbers, so Codex's speed scaling and
  footstep timing are unchanged:
  - **idle** is now calm: breathing, weight shift, slow glances, no snapping;
  - **walk** is upright: arms swinging against the legs, shoulders counter-rotating, hips rolling.
- **New clips:** `turn`, `look_around`, `panel_check`, `records_read`, `listen_brief`,
  `investigate`, `capture`.
- **Investigating:** the Nurse's `search` and `lost` states now present as `investigate`, a careful
  walk with her head sweeping smoothly. Older GLBs fall back to the old `search`.

### 2.2 Model fixes (found when the arms first lifted)
The old clips never lifted her arms. The new ones do, and that exposed two defects, both fixed:

| Defect | Cause | Fix | Measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lifting the arms tore the dress at the hips. | The clasped forearms rested on the pelvis in the sculpt, so dress and apron vertices took arm weights. | Gave the arms clearance in the sculpt: elbows out, hands forward. Same pose and silhouette. Forearm and hand weights are kept off the torso below the chest, and hip accessories stay on the hips. | Stretched edges in the capture pose: **157 → 0** |
| A web of skin between her hands. | Her fingers crossed the centre line, so the remesh fused both hands. | Each hand now stays on its own side; still clasped. | (included above) |
| Face read as a dark mass. | Dark grey-brown skin (0.26). | Pale, waxy skin (0.5). | — |

### 2.3 Runtime hook (presentation interface, no gameplay logic)
`EntityVisual` gains an **optional** `playClip(name | null, { fade, restart })` and a `clip`
read-out (`{ name, time, duration, finished }`):
- **Model visual:** plays the authored clip with a cross-fade until `null` hands control back to
  state-driven playback. Gait clips are still time-scaled by speed and still emit footsteps.
- **Procedural body (load fallback):** plays the clip's documented fallback state.
- **No call:** without a `playClip` call, behaviour is exactly as before.

## 3. Animation contract (Phase 3)

All clips are **in place**: the hips travel under 8 cm (tested), and nothing moves the actor.
**Gameplay navigation and yaw are authoritative.** Every clip starts and ends in, or blends from,
the standing idle pose unless noted. Clips sample at 30 fps.

| Clip | Duration | Loop | Entry → exit | Root motion | Target binding | Safe fallback | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `idle` | 8.0 s | loop | idle | none | — | `idle` | Standing still. |
| `walk` | 2.333 s (2 cycles) | loop | idle ↔ walk | none; nominal 0.9 m/s, stride 1.05 m, 4 footsteps | — | `walk` | Patrol and authored walking. Time-scaled by speed (0.35–2.2×). |
| `turn` | 1.2 s | loop | idle → idle | none; gameplay rotates her | — | `idle` | Turning on the spot (little or no translation). |
| `look_around` | 4.2 s | one-shot (holds last frame) | idle → idle | none | — | `idle` | Glance left, then right. |
| `panel_check` | 5.0 s | one-shot | idle → idle | none | `nw:station:nurse-panel` | `observe` | 0.3–1.1 s looks up-left at the annunciator; 2.4–3.2 s reaches; 3.2–3.9 s presses reset; ends at idle. |
| `records_read` | 6.0 s | loop | idle (fade ~0.6 s) → idle | none | `nw:station:nurse-records` | `idle` | Leaning over the Room 104 record; turns a page at 3 s. |
| `listen_brief` | 2.4 s | one-shot | any → idle | none | — | `listen` | A restrained listen: the head turns slowly. Use the old `listen` only for the uncanny version. |
| `investigate` | 5.818 s (4 cycles) | loop | walk ↔ investigate | none; nominal 0.55 m/s, stride 0.8 m, 8 footsteps | — | `search` | Careful search walk. Automatic for the `search` and `lost` states. |
| `capture` | 1.4 s | one-shot (clamps) | any → held reach | none | — | `alert` | Arms reach to shoulder height, face level with the player, head tipped. Place her about 0.9 m in front of the camera and fade about 0.15 s. |
| `listen`, `alert`, `run`, `search` | 6 / 0.6 / 1.18 / 5.71 s | as before | — | none | — | — | Unchanged, kept for existing callers. |

The **footstep events** for `walk` and `investigate` come from authored contacts, through the
existing `EntityVisual.update` return value. `turn` has no step events.

## 4. Station staging (Phase 4)

**Art** (Blender, `src/world/dressing/pipeline/station_set.py`, rebuilt):
- **Annunciator:** reads `NURSE CALL · WARD 4 WEST` with lamps 101–108. **Room 104's lamp is the
  lit one** (it was in the 102 slot).
- **Nurse-call master station** on the back desk: a raked keypad (CALL, CANCEL, RESET), a handset on
  its cradle, and a faint green display reading **"CALL RM 104 / 03:17 WAITING"**.
- **The Room 104 record:** a buff folder labelled `ROOM 104 — OBSERVATIONS` with a red NBM
  (nil-by-mouth) sticker. Its obs chart is pulled half out: entries run 20:00 to 03:00, then
  **03:17 circled in red, with nothing written after it**. That's surface print only; any inspection
  text is Codex's.
- The print uses its own lit label material and the display an emissive one; neither is baked.
- **Budget:** 29,922 triangles, 1,446,924 bytes (1.38 MiB), 5 materials; the test budget is
  < 40k / < 2 MiB.

**Lighting cue for the hiding place:** a torch lies dropped on the floor in front of the staff
lockers, still on (`addStationPractical`). Its low, cool pool lights the hiding locker's door from
below, so the eye goes there with no marker and no prompt. It's visual only, with no collider.

It's added last in the world build, so its fixture takes the next lamp number and **no existing
fixture id or flicker seed changes**. It's on circuit `nw:lights:station-torch` (plus the automatic
`all` and room circuits).

**Semantic bindings** (`src/world/stealth/layout.ts` `STATION_INTERACTIONS`, nodes in
`nursesStation.ts`, typed accessor **`getStationStaging(world)`**):

| Node | What | Data |
|---|---|---|
| `nw:station:nurse-panel` | Where she works the nurse-call panel | `stand` (−4.4, 0, −5.42), `yaw` 0 (facing −Z), `clip` `panel_check`, `target` = reset key, `lookAt` = lit 104 lamp |
| `nw:station:nurse-records` | Where she reads the record | `stand` (−3.8, 0, −5.42), `yaw` 0, `clip` `records_read`, `target` = record |
| `nw:station:call-console` | The reset key | prop point |
| `nw:station:call-lamp-104` | The lit lamp (art node `station_call_led`) | prop point |
| `nw:station:record-104` | The Room 104 record | prop point |

**Measured on the real physics** (`tests/world/stealth/nursesStation.test.ts`):
- Both stand points are free for her body (r 0.3 m) and supported.
- Both are reachable from her route (`patrol-0`, `patrol-1`) and back.
- Her hand reach is 0.57 m to the console and 0.51 m to the record.
- **While she works the panel, the staff locker is 136° off her facing**, against a 49.5° half field
  of view. The locker is behind her shoulder, which is the hiding opportunity.

**Nothing changes stealth visibility.** No collider was added, moved or removed (all the new
art is visual), the trolley cover and the Room 105 hiding are untouched, and the NW-006A stealth
tests pass unchanged.

## 5. Audio cues (Phase 5)

These are new synthesized cues in `src/atmosphere/ambience.ts`. All are **positional** when given a
position, are played through the existing `atmosphere:sound` event, and stop with `stopAll`. Levels
are tested on a recording AudioContext: every cue is under 0.6× the chase stinger, `nw:capture` is
under 0.5×, and her heels are no louder than the existing entity heel click.

| Cue | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `nw:shaw-steps` | A ward sister's heels on vinyl, approaching; keys now and then. | `duration` = seconds of steps (default 5), one every ~0.62 s, rising gently. For off-screen approach. |
| `nw:shaw-keys` | The chatelaine settling. | For stops, turns and reaching. |
| `nw:shaw-rustle` | Starched cotton swish. | For turning, reaching, bending to listen. |
| `nw:call-panel` | Key click, acknowledged call tone, reset bip. | Play at the `panel_check` press (~3.2 s). |
| `nw:paper` | A page turned in a folder. | Play at the `records_read` page turn (~3 s). |
| `nw:station-hum` | Mains hum and a tired monitor fan. | Use `loop: true`; seamless at any `duration`. |
| `nw:capture` | A low push, a close breath, one cold tone (1.6 s). | Restrained: the image is the scare. |

Her **voice** (Codex's temporary lines) is untouched, and no dialogue was added.

## 6. Integration notes for Codex (NW-007A / NW-007C)

1. **Authored actions:** move her with your navigation to `getStationStaging(world).panel.stand`,
   set her yaw to `.yaw`, then `visual.playClip('panel_check')`. Watch `visual.clip.finished`, then
   `visual.playClip(null)`. Records work the same way, with `records_read` (a loop: end it when your
   beat ends).
2. **Replace the stand-in:** `forceState('observe')` and `checkingYaw` from `patrol[2]` in
   `stationBindings.ts` can be replaced by the staging accessor, which removes the 5.5 m long-range
   panel check.
3. **One actor:** `playClip` is presentation only; it never moves the actor or touches its capsule.
   Keep using the same visual instance across the authored → autonomous handoff.
4. **Investigating** needs no call: `search` and `lost` already play `investigate`.
5. **Sound:** swap `nw:distant-steps` → `nw:shaw-steps`, `nw:static` (at the panel) →
   `nw:call-panel`, and `nw:curtain` (when she listens) → `nw:shaw-rustle`. The others are new and
   optional.
6. **Capture:** `playClip('capture', { fade: 0.15 })` with her about 0.9 m from the camera, plus
   `nw:capture`.
7. **Fallbacks:** if `nurse.glb` fails to load, the procedural body plays each clip's fallback state.
   `playClip` returns `false` for an unknown clip and leaves playback alone.
8. **Older callers:** the Nurse's clip list grew. Code that iterates manifest clips still works, and
   `listen`, `alert`, `run` and `search` are kept.

## 8. Blender sources and rebuild

The sources are the generator scripts; `.blend` files are build outputs (git-ignored `.work`, as in
NW-005A and NW-006A).

- **Nurse:**
  - files: `src/horror/entities/pipeline/blender/nurse.py` (sculpt), `rig_and_export.py` (rig,
    weights, bake, export), and `src/horror/entities/nurseClips.ts` with
    `pipeline/samplePoses.test.ts` (clips);
  - rebuild just the Nurse:
    ```
    PY=<python with bpy>; W=<work dir>
    $PY src/horror/entities/pipeline/blender/nurse.py -- $W/sculpt
    NW_POSE_ENTITY=nurse NW_POSE_OUT=$W/poses.json npx vitest run src/horror/entities/pipeline/samplePoses.test.ts
    (cd src/horror/entities/pipeline/blender && $PY rig_and_export.py -- nurse $W/sculpt/nurse.blend $W/poses.json $W/game)
    ```
    then copy `$W/game/nurse.glb` to `src/horror/entities/assets/` and replace the `nurse` entry of
    `manifest.json` with `$W/game/nurse.manifest.json`. `build_all.sh` still rebuilds every entity.
- **Station:** `BLENDER_PY=<python with bpy> src/world/dressing/pipeline/build_station.sh`.

## 9. Known limitations and NW-008 items

These are **not fixed here**, because they need a head and model rebuild, which the brief reserves
for NW-008:

1. **Nurse head assembly (highest priority for NW-008).** In the exported GLB, the blindfold, cap and
   hair sit **beside and behind the face instead of on it**, and the face and neck albedo is
   **streaked black and white**. Both are present in the baseline asset (`0097264`) as well:
   `docs/nw-007b/audit/nw008-head-baseline-0097264.png` and `nw008-head-nw007b.png`. The sculpt
   renders correctly, so the defect is in the game export and bake of the head parts. I tried two
   targeted fixes:
   - rigid head weighting, which made no change in the rest pose, showing the displacement is
     geometric, not skinning;
   - limiting the skin's crevice AO during the colour bake, which made no visible change.

   I reverted both. **At a distance and in motion she reads correctly. Close up (capture), her face
   does not.**
2. **Long neck, tube limbs, mitten hands:** a stylised sculpt. That's NW-008.
3. **No hand-held props.** She reads the record on the desk rather than holding a chart. Props on
   bones are an NW-008 item.
4. **`turn` has no footstep events.** Gameplay may play `nw:shaw-steps` quietly during long turns.
5. **The torch is on the mains circuits too** (the builder always adds `all` and the room circuit),
   so a brownout dims a battery torch.
6. **Not human-verified.** No playtest, and no real-GPU frame timing. Every image is from SwiftShader
   or Blender Cycles.
7. **Clip previews** are rendered stills assembled into GIFs (12 frames per clip), not real-time
   captures.
