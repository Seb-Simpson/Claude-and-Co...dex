# NW-006A — Nurses’ Station environment and stealth layout

**Status: draft for integration. Do not merge automatically.**

This is the first stealth space in Chapter 1: the player should learn to read Nurse Shaw's route,
use cover and understand the station before the danger is forced on them. This PR builds the
space, its bindings and the tests for it. It does **not** implement enemy AI, detection, or
progression (those are Codex's).

## 2. The space

All positions are game coordinates (metres, +Y up, north = −Z), authored once in
`src/world/stealth/layout.ts`.

```
            N (−Z)
   x −9.0          −6.4 .. −1.55 station (counter z −2.45..−1.5)        x 4.6
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   observation  ●  ║   locker  ▮  ·  ·  ◆ patrol   ║  gap x≈−1..−0.6  ║
   (−9.0,0.45)     ║            (−6.4,−4.15)       ║   east corridor   ║   exit ▶ (4.6,0.2)
   corridor  ──────╫────────  counter  ───────────╫───────────────────────────────
                  entry  ●(−4.2,0.2)     crouch-cover ●(−4.6,−0.42) behind screen ▮(−4.6,−1.0)
```

| Element | Position | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Entry** | (−4.2, 0, 0.2) | Player enters from the corridor approach. |
| **Observation** | (−9.0, 0, 0.45) | Watch the nurse through the open counter. Unseen while she is in the station (§4). |
| **Crouch cover** | (−4.6, 0, −0.42) | Stand here and crouch: the 1.2 m linen screen hides you from the station's west and centre. |
| **Linen screen** | centre (−4.6, 0.6, −1.0), 0.9 × 1.2 × 0.5 m | A deliberate solid collider (+1 to the pinned count, §6). |
| **Hiding (primary)** | `nw:hiding:nurses-staff`, the staff locker inside the station | Obvious and close to her route. |
| **Patrol** | `nw:route:station-round` (existing, Codex's) | Ten waypoints through the station, out to the corridor and back. Every leg is walkable on her agent. |
| **Exit** | (4.6, 0, 0.2) | East along the corridor, past the treatment door, toward the next section. |
| **Doors** | `nw:door:treatment` | On the route. |
| **Audio** | `trolley` emitter at (−1.6, 0.9, −5.2), `intercom` emitter at (−3.4, 1.9, −5.9) | The trolley sounds ahead of her; the intercom is the station's call panel. |

### Dressing
- **Station interior:** the existing station (counter, monitors, ward board, filing shelves,
  noticeboard with papers, staff locker bank, clock, lamp) is unchanged.
- **Lighting:** the station has its own working ceiling tube and a desk lamp (tested). The corridor
  is dim; the station is the readable place.
- **Not done:** no new Blender props for this space (see §8).

## 3. Scene bindings (for Codex)

Every point is a **uniquely named node** under `world.root`, with `userData.nwStealth` describing
it. Read them through the typed accessor. Do not copy coordinates.

```ts
import { getNursesStationStealth } from './src/world/stealth/nursesStation';
const s = getNursesStationStealth(world); // throws if a binding is missing
s.entry.position            // { x, y, z }
s.observation.position
s.crouchCover.position
s.screen.position           // node at the screen's centre; size in node.userData.nwStealth.size
s.patrolWaypoints           // ordered, 10 entries, route nw:route:station-round
s.patrolRouteId             // 'nw:route:station-round'
s.hidingPrimary             // 'nw:hiding:nurses-staff'
s.exit.position
s.doorIds                   // ['nw:door:treatment']
s.emitterIds                // two sound-emitter ids (trolley, intercom)
```

Node names: `nw:stealth:entry`, `nw:stealth:observation`, `nw:stealth:crouch-cover`,
`nw:stealth:screen`, `nw:stealth:patrol-0` … `nw:stealth:patrol-9`, `nw:stealth:exit`.

## 4. Design rules Codex must honour

These are what the measurements show. They are not decoration.

1. **Crouch works from the station's west side, not the east.** Measured with the real line of
   sight (eye heights: standing 1.65 m, crouched 0.9 m; the nurse's 1.65 m): a crouched player at
   the crouch cover is hidden from the station waypoints `patrol-0` (−3.6,−4.6) and `patrol-1`
   (−1.5,−4.9), but **seen from the east gap** (`patrol-2`/`patrol-8`, (−0.8,−3.2)). A standing
   player is seen from all station waypoints. That is the lesson: cover is directional.
2. **The observation point is safe only while she is inside the station.** From the station
   waypoints the player at observation is **7.4 m, 9.2 m or 9.0 m** away (`patrol-0`, `patrol-1`,
   `patrol-2`). Her vision range is 7 m: no station waypoint is within it. Only `patrol-2` has a
   line of sight to the player, and it is 9.0 m away. **But her corridor leg (`patrol-3`…`patrol-5`) walks
   west past the observation point (`patrol-5` is 1.6 m from it).** The player must leave before
   she turns onto the corridor. Codex should tell this to the player by her movement, not by a
   marker.
3. **The corridor is her route.** Anywhere in the corridor west of x ≈ −0.75 is on her leg. The
   east corridor (x > 0) is off her route and is the safe way out.

## 7. Integration instructions for Codex

1. **Base.** Merge PR #17 first (or stack on it). Then pull NW-006A.
2. **NW-005C (PR #19).** It lacks the NW-005A environment commits after `353ce4d`: the ceiling
   collapse remodel (`a6de4d5`), the zone and fill changes in `src/atmosphere/`, the emergency and
   dying-tube lighting in `src/world/rooms/shell.ts` and `service.ts`, and the polish commits.
   Those must come across before #19 is final, or the opening will not look like the approved one.
3. **Spawning the nurse.** The default world spawns only the patient (`activeEntityTypes:
   ['patient']` in `application.ts`). The stealth encounter needs the **nurse spawned**. Her spawn
   and route are in `metadata.ts` (`nw:spawn:nurse`, route `nw:route:station-round`); this PR does
   not change them.
4. **Behaviour.** Enemy AI, detection and the encounter script are yours. Use `getNursesStationStealth`
   for every position; do not hard-code them.
5. **Sound.** The `trolley` and `intercom` emitters are ambient cues only. A nurse-presence cue
   (heels, keys, humming) is Codex's, and should use the trolley emitter's position if it wants
   the sound to come from the station.
6. **Doors.** `nw:door:treatment` is on the route. The station has no door of its own in this layout.

## 8. Known limitations (be honest about these)

- **Blender was not used for this space.** The brief asks for the Blender pipeline. The station
  interior is the existing procedural station (it looks well made, but it is procedural). The
  **linen screen is a procedural block** that reads as grey steel, not as a laden linen trolley.
  A proper trolley model, and filing cabinets and supply cupboards for the station, are the next
  step and are not done.
- **Only one hiding location.** The brief asks for a second, optional hiding place. The crouch cover
  is an optional *position*, but not a second hiding spot. A second locker needs floor space the
  station does not have without moving existing furniture. Not done.
- **The nurse is not in the default game** (§7.3), so no screenshot shows her. The views show the
  space, not the encounter.
- **Observation is timed, not permanent.** See rule 2. A player who stays at observation when she
  turns into the corridor will be on her route. This is a design choice the brief did not forbid,
  but it needs a playtest.
- **Crouch cover is directional** (rule 1). Some players will expect cover from every side.
- **No real-GPU measurement.** All renders and costs are from the software renderer.
- **Not playtested.** Whether the space reads without objective markers is unverified.
- The station's **hanging sign and room plaques** are unchanged procedural canvas text.
