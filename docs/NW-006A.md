## 2. What was built

### Blender station set (`src/world/dressing/pipeline/station_set.py`)
A second, small Blender set built, baked and exported in one pass (`build_station.sh`). It loads
alongside the Room 104 set and falls back on its own (`src/world/dressing/station.ts`). Every prop is a
named node, so tests can check each one against its collider. All props share **one baked atlas**:
albedo with AO from the station’s walls, desk and counter, plus ORM and normal maps. The station’s own
troffer and desk lamp light them at runtime.

| Prop | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Covered linen trolley** (replaces the grey block) | The crouch cover. A stainless trolley on castors with folded sheets, pillowcases, blankets and gowns. A fitted cover runs over the **station side, both ends and the top**, and the front panel is rolled up. The art shows which side the cover works from: from the corridor you see open shelves; from the station you see the cover. Its footprint is exactly the 0.9 × 1.2 × 0.5 m collider (tested). |
| **Records corner** (north-west) | Two four-drawer filing cabinets; one drawer is left open on hanging files. A clinical supply cupboard stands with one door ajar on gloves, dressings and syringe boxes, its padlock hanging open. Archive boxes and a lever-arch file sit on top. |
| **Nurse-call annunciator** | Above the cabinets, with eight room lamps. **Room 104’s is still lit** and blinks slowly: a call nobody answered. It is the only emissive in the set and too dim to light the wall. |
| **Observations machine** | Parked by the east wall: five-star base, cuff in the basket, hose and probe cable hanging. |
| **Glove dispenser** | On the east wall beside the way in. |
| **Paperwork** | Back desk: lever-arch files, a stack of patient notes, an obs chart and pen, a cold mug of tea. Counter: the drug-round folder left open, a sharps bin, a box of gloves. |
| **Contact shadows** | A soft dark quad under each floor prop (one draw call). |

**Lighting.** No lights were added or changed. The station keeps its buzzing troffer, the dead troffer
over the east half and the warm desk lamp. The east side and the records corner are therefore the
darker parts, and they read clearly (§5).

### Procedural stand-ins and colliders (`src/world/rooms/stealthStation.ts`)
Simple blocks are shown only if the station set fails to load. They are suppressed through
`STATION_REGIONS` (`regions.ts`) when it loads. The colliders are identical either way.

| Exit | `nw:stealth:exit` | (4.6, 0, 0.2) |

| Audio | `trolley` emitter (−1.6, 0.9, −5.2), `intercom` emitter (−3.4, 1.9, −5.9) | unchanged |

**Why Room 105.** The brief asked for a second place only if the geometry supported it naturally.
Three candidates were measured with the real physics:

- **Room 108**, across from the east gap: **rejected**. Its door stands ajar at 32°, and the navigation
  path from the corridor is `null`, so it is impassable without changing the door system.
- **A new locker in the station:** not needed, and the station has no floor space for one without
  moving furniture.
- **Room 105: chosen.** Its door already stands open beside the observation point, and it already
  holds Codex’s under-bed hiding. It is a **4.53 m** walk from the observation point.

One finding matters for how 105 is used: **the open floor of 105 is not safe.** Sampled along her
whole route, 14 points can see a standing player beside the bed as she walks past the door. Only the
hiding conceals the player. This also answers the first pass’s weakness: the observation point is
safe only while she is in the station. When she turns onto the corridor, the player has somewhere
to go.

Timing (from her profile and the player config): she patrols at 0.75 m/s. From the east gap she needs
about **9 s** to reach 105’s door, and the player needs about **1.9 s** at 2.4 m/s to reach the bed.

Read every position through the accessor; never copy coordinates:

```ts
const s = getNursesStationStealth(world);
s.hidingPrimary        // 'nw:hiding:nurses-staff'
s.hidingSecondary      // 'nw:hiding:room-105-bed-b'
s.concealment.position // approach point beside bed B in Room 105
```

## 5. Browser evidence (`tests/browser/nw-006a-stealth.spec.ts`)

The spec ran in the playable game on the PR #19 integration, with the opening cinematic skipped as
Codex’s specs do. Result: **passing** (4.0 min).

- Both sets load (`station_set.glb` 200, *“Nurses’ Station set loaded”*, no fallback warning).
- **Keyboard walk:** from the east gap, deep into the station and west across it, ending at (−3.46, −4.70).
- **13 player views** are in `docs/nw-006a/shots/`:
  - approach and observation;
  - the trolley from the corridor and from the station;
  - the crouch cover, **standing and crouched**;
  - the records corner, back desk, east side, east-gap entry and glove dispenser;
  - Room 105, where Codex’s **“E — Hide under bed”** prompt appears;
  - the exit.

  Codex’s staged first-sighting nurse stands in the corridor in the approach and observation views.
- **Readability without markers.** Measured on each full frame as sRGB luma:
  - medians are 0.10–0.21;
  - near-black (below 0.04) is at most 18% (the dark corridor at the observation point) and 2–6% inside the station;
  - clipped highlights are at most 0.3%.

  The station is the readable island in a dark ward. Whether players read it unprompted still needs a playtest.
- **Regression:** Codex’s `foundation.spec.ts` (two tests) and `nw-005d-readability.spec.ts` pass with the station set loaded.

The spec sets `--enable-unsafe-swiftshader` for its own file only. Without it, this container’s
software renderer advanced the simulation by about 0.05 s per real second, too slow for keyboard
walking (measured). The shared Playwright config is unchanged.

## 8. Integration notes for Codex (NW-006B)

1. Use `getNursesStationStealth(world)` for every position. `hidingSecondary` and `concealment` are new.
2. Cover is directional by design: the east gap sees past the trolley. If NW-006B teaches crouching,
   bring her into the station from the west side first.
3. The observation point is timed. When she turns onto the corridor leg, the intended answer is
   Room 105’s bed (about 9 s against 1.9 s).
4. Room 104’s blinking call lamp is the node `station_call_led` (material `call_led`). If an encounter
   beat wants to switch it off, hide that node.
5. The trolley and intercom emitters are unchanged; any nurse-presence sound is yours.

## 9. Remaining limitations

- **Not playtested.** Readability is measured from frames, not from players.
- **No real-GPU measurement.**
- The station’s **existing procedural pieces** (counter interior, chairs, shelving, glass cabinet,
  lockers, desk, monitors) are not remodelled in Blender. The new Blender props sit beside them and
  are lit the same way, but the shelving and glass cabinet are visibly simpler.
- The new props are **not in the Room 104 light map**: no bounce light. They are grounded by baked AO
  and contact shadows, and lit by the station’s real lights.
- **The second concealment is an existing hiding place**, reused rather than authored. Its approach is
  visible from the corridor (measured), so the player must commit to hiding.
- The crouched view (`05`) is close to the trolley by nature: at 0.9 m eye height, 0.33 m from it, the
  player sees the shelves, not the station.
- Codex’s **NW-005A/B/C browser specs were not re-run** here; the NW-005D and foundation specs were.
