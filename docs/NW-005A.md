## 2. Existing architecture (reused, not replaced)

```
hospital.module.ts setup()
  ├─ loadRoom104Set()                 src/world/dressing/room104.ts (GLB + baked light map)
  ├─ createHospitalWorld({ suppressVisuals: PROTOTYPE_SUPPRESSION })
  │     procedural builders (src/world/rooms/*)
  │     colliders, doors, interactables, hiding, spawns, lights, anomalies
  │     builder.add() skips static visuals fully inside the regions in dressing/regions.ts
  ├─ world.root.add(set.root)         visual-only Blender set
  └─ wheelchair: procedural meshes hidden, Blender wheelchair parented into the anomaly group
```

- **Blender pipeline:** `src/world/dressing/pipeline/`. It covers textures, modelling, the atlas and light-map bake, the GLB export and the optimiser. It runs headless on the `bpy` 5.2 module.
- **Doors:** procedural, built by `architecture.ts door()`. Gameplay rotates the door group, and its kinematic collider follows (`src/gameplay/interactions.ts`).
- **Lights:** the atmosphere module's dynamic, unshadowed lights. They flicker and follow profiles.
- **Baked light:** the set's light map is applied as an `aoMap` that also scales direct light, which stands in for real-time shadows.
- **Gameplay interfaces:**
  - `HospitalWorld` (contracts);
  - `getHospitalExtras(world)`;
  - Codex's `ChapterEnvironmentBindings` / `validateChapterBindings(world, bindings, { anchors })`. It accepts data anchors (`{id, position}`) or uniquely named `Object3D` nodes under `world.root`.

**What is already good and stays:**

- the Room 104 furniture set, bedding and wheelchair;
- the rot decals;
- the light-map approach;
- all colliders, doors and interactions;
- the `?nwSet=procedural` fallback.

**What reads as a blockout today (baseline screenshots in `docs/nw-005a/baseline/`):**

- flat procedural door leaves and box frames;
- the corridor east of x −7 (Room 106's side, the connector, the station approach) has untextured procedural finishes;
- the station counter front;
- flat corridor lighting with no composition;
- no card reader, access card or intercom in the opening.

## 3. Integration risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Visual-only changes drifting from collision | The set never adds or moves colliders. `regions.test.ts` keeps asserting that every gameplay record is identical with and without suppression. New props are wall-mounted or placed on existing collision. |
| Replacing door visuals while gameplay animates the door group | The Blender leaf is parented inside the same door group (as the wheelchair already is), and only the procedural leaf meshes are hidden. Colliders and the swept checks are untouched. |
| Suppression catching the wrong pieces | Suppression boxes are per-door frame boxes plus the extended corridor-face boxes. A test lists exactly what is suppressed. |
| Codex hard-coding coordinates | All opening references are published once, in `src/world/opening.ts`, as data plus named nodes. The IDs are stable and positions are read from the world. |
| The rejected Chapter 1 layout (#14, reverted by #16) coming back through a merge | Main's tree after the revert is identical to this branch's base, so the merge had no conflicts and brought in no layout code. Checked: no `chapter1` layout files, switch or tests exist on this branch. |
| GPU budget on integrated graphics | Shared atlases, one leaf mesh reused for all doors, a 2048 light map, and KTX-free JPEG textures as before. Draw calls and triangles are measured in the browser. |
| Software rendering here (SwiftShader) | Screenshots and timings are relative evidence only. Real-GPU frame rate is listed as unverified. |

## 5. Scene bindings (for gameplay)

`src/world/opening.ts` publishes the bindings in two forms, with stable IDs:

- **As data:** `getHospitalExtras(world).opening`.
- **As uniquely named `Object3D` nodes under `world.root`:** the form Codex's `validateChapterBindings()` already resolves.

Gameplay should read positions from these, never from copied coordinates.

| Key | Node | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `player-spawn` | `nw:opening:player-spawn` | Spawn (−12.4, 0, 4.1). Yaw 0 looks at the open door; camera pitch 0. Same as `world.playerSpawn`. |
| `room-104-door` | `nw:opening:room-104-door` | Door `nw:door:room-104`, with its stand point. The only exit; gameplay owns its state. |
| `card-reader` | `nw:opening:card-reader` | Reader face at 1.15 m on the room side, latch side of the door. Stand point at arm's length. |
| `access-card` | `nw:opening:access-card` | Pickup location (floor). The visual is `nw:opening:access-card-prop` in the set: hide it on pickup. |
| `intercom-speaker` | `nw:opening:intercom-speaker` | Source for the `c1.voice.intercom.*` lines in Room 104. |
| `room-106-door` | `nw:opening:room-106-door` | Door `nw:door:room-106`, with its stand point. |
| `room-106-handle` | `nw:opening:room-106-handle` | **Child of the 106 door group**, so it moves with the leaf. Starts on the corridor face. |
| `nurse-first-sight` | `nw:opening:nurse-first-sight` | Nurse Shaw's first appearance: at the station end, facing back toward 104, 10.8 m away. |
| `nurse-first-sight-view` | `nw:opening:nurse-first-sight-view` | The view she is first seen from (stepping out of 104). Line of sight tested. |
| `station-approach` | `nw:opening:station-approach` | Walkable path from the bed to the station lobby (tested with the player body). |

## 6. Integration points changed

All of these are in my files.

- **`hospital.module.ts`:** after the set loads, `dressDoors(world, set.doorLeaf)`. On dispose, `restore()`.
- **`createHospitalWorld.ts`:** `addOpeningMarkers(builder)` adds the named nodes and the intercom emitter, and `extras.opening` carries the bindings. No collider, door, interactable, spawn or route changes. `regions.test.ts` asserts that every gameplay record is identical with and without the set.
- **`dressing/regions.ts`:** corridor regions extended to x −0.2, plus one frame box per dressed door. East of x −7 the face boxes are thin, so the visitors' chairs and sanitisers stay procedural.
- **`atmosphere/ambience.ts`:** cue list for the `intercom` emitter kind.

## 7. Blender sources and rebuilding

- **Sources:** the pipeline scripts in `src/world/dressing/pipeline/` (`textures.py`, `room104_set.py`, `opening.py`, `bake_export.py`, `optimize_glb.py`). The set is generated deterministically from them.
- **Build:** `BLENDER_PY=<python with bpy 5.x> src/world/dressing/pipeline/build_set.sh` takes about 25 minutes on 4 CPU cores. `--quick` takes about 1 minute.
- **Saved files:** the modelled `.blend` from this build is `docs/nw-005a/blender/room104_opening_set.blend`. Its images are external; regenerate them with `textures.py`.

## 9. Final polish (10 Nov)

**Design direction applied:** Chapter 1 opens with exploration and uncertainty. Room 104 is safe enough to look round; the corridor is where the hospital starts to feel wrong; nothing is a scripted scare.

| Area | Problem found from the player's viewpoint | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Wet-floor sign | Stood as a V (panels tilted the wrong way). | A-frame, tops meeting under the handle, print upright (`shots/12-wet-floor-sign.png`). |
| Access card | Rendered almost black: it shared a baked material and sampled the light map with no light-map UVs. | Its own material, lit by the room. A pale grey lanyard loops round it and leads the eye. It lies under Room 104's working tube, between the bed and the door. No emissive, no outline. |
| Room 104 | As dark as every other patient room, with a flickering tube. | Its tube works (a faint buzz, no flicker), and the room has its own brighter mood (`player-start`: ambient 0.3 against 0.19). The unease starts at the door, where the corridor tube stutters. |
| Card reader | A black box; its face could not be read. | A small steady red status LED, as on a real locked reader. It is the one point of colour on that wall. |
| Steel | Door plates, corner guards and the bed frame rendered black. Cycles bakes no base colour for pure metals, and the engine has no environment map for them to reflect. | Base colour is baked with metalness off, and metalness is scaled to 0.4 in game. Steel reads as worn grey steel. |
| Ceiling | Stains repeated identically on every tile (the texture is one tile). | The tile texture is evenly aged; stains come only from the water-stain decals. |
| Restraint | Ash handprints by 106 and on the counter, and rot on the corridor ceiling, were overtly supernatural. | Removed. One patch of rot by 104's door and old scratches by 106 remain. |
| Station as destination | From the 104 doorway the far end of the corridor was nearly black. The game lights only the 10 nearest lamps; lamps behind closed doors and faint monitor glows took the slots, and the working tube over the station approach was faded to 6 %. | Lamps on corridor sightlines and at the station count as nearer, and brighter lamps outrank faint ones (`lightRig.ts`). That tube is brighter with a longer reach, and corridor fog is lighter (0.05). Measured in the browser from the doorway, the mean brightness of the far end rose from 8 to 25 (of 255), and its 95th percentile from 25 to 75. See `shots/05-step-out-east.png`. |

**After review feedback (the yellow block in the ceiling; "good lighting but not scary"):**

| Area | Problem | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Ceiling collapse | The insulation was a single rounded yellow box. | Remodelled: a bent T-bar, half the tile hanging by its edge, two sagging mineral-wool batts in the hole, a torn membrane and a foil strip hanging down, thin grey and red cables ending in a broken connector (`shots/13-ceiling-collapse.png`, `13b`). The hanging membrane bakes very dark and reads as black sheet. |
| Corridor lighting | Evenly lit and warm: readable, not frightening. | The tube outside 104 is dying (dark, with brief stutters) and the west end's is dead, so the only working tube is over the station. Emergency luminaires are a weak, sickly green with a buzz instead of steady amber. The broken connector arcs now and then and lights the hole in bursts. Corridor ambient 0.2 → 0.13, and the camera-following fill is weaker (0.22 → 0.12 base), so the dark is no longer flattened. Bake lights match. |

The corridor is now mostly dark with three kinds of light: the green emergency glow, the intermittent stutter and arc, and the lit station at the end. Room 104 is unchanged by this pass.

**Inspected and left as they are:**

- **Scale.** Doors are 1.2 × 2.1 m openings with 1.1 m leaves; the handrail is at 0.92 m; the counter ledge is at 1.12 m. These match the colliders and UK ward practice.
- **Clipping.** None found in the 13 final views or in the close-up inspection shots of the open 104 door leaf, the door frames and closer, the card reader, the sink and wardrobe, and the counter. I did not inspect every prop from every angle.
- **Repetition.** The wainscot's scratch pattern repeats every 1.2 m. It is low contrast and I left it.
- **Still procedural.** The station's interior (desk items, the monitors' flat bright screens, the back wall), the room-number plaques, the light panels and the west window.

**Preserved:** no collider, door, interactable, spawn, route or binding changed in the polish (`regions.test.ts`, `opening.test.ts`). The set is 100,296 triangles and 9.2 MB, inside the 110 k and 9 MiB budgets.

## 10. Known limitations

- **The west end of the corridor is now very dark** (`shots/06-corridor-west.png`): shapes and the moonlit window read, detail does not. That is intended, but it should be checked on a real display.

- **No real GPU.** All rendering evidence comes from SwiftShader. Colour, flicker timing and frame rate need a check on real hardware.
- **The access card is small.** It is a correctly sized 86 mm card. After the final polish it is lit by the room and has a pale lanyard looped round it, and it is visible from the wake position (`shots/04b-card-from-wake.png`). It has no glow; whether players notice it without a prompt needs a playtest.
- **Some visuals are still procedural.** The station interior (desk items, back wall, the monitors' bright screens), the room plaques and hanging sign, the light panels, and the west window.
- **The wet-floor sign has no collider,** as before, so the player can walk through it. Adding one would change the collider count Codex's tests pin.
- **Static bake.** The baked light assumes these tube states. If gameplay turns tubes on later, the baked shadows stay as they are.
- **Gameplay is not implemented here.** Card pickup, the reader's accept/deny logic, intercom playback and the Nurse's first appearance are Codex's. The bindings say where these happen.
- **The Chapter 1 layout (#14) was rejected** (merged, then reverted by #16). This work does not depend on it and contains none of it.
