# NW-004A — Chapter 1 level design (proposal v2, for approval)

**Status: design for approval. No geometry has been rebuilt.** Built on Codex's Chapter 1
package (draft PR #13, `docs/nw-004/*`, `src/gameplay/chapter/bindings.ts`). The earlier
Fractured Ward and concept-atrium proposals are withdrawn (they remain in git history).

**Principle: design the story, the Nurse's first appearance and the Patient chase first, then
shape the hospital around them.** The result is a small old British infirmary floor. It keeps the
current footprint, adds one narrow staff passage, and re-purposes existing rooms so that:

* every act has a reason to be where it is;
* there are three parallel routes (ward side, staff passage, service passage) instead of one
  corridor;
* every view except one is broken within about 10 m.

## 1. The building

**Halden Vale Infirmary, West Ward (Ward 4 West).**

* **1930s:** a long ward. That is why the patient wing is a straight corridor ending in a tall
  window.
* **1970s:** the ward was split into single rooms. The old **day room** survives as the ward hall:
  a taller ceiling with a roof lantern, a longcase clock stopped at 03:17 and a dead upright piano.
* **2011:** the Night Observation Programme took over the east end of the ward:
  - an observation bay;
  - a viewing room (the observation gallery);
  - a release vestibule;
  - a **retention barrier retrofitted across the staff stair (Stairwell B)**.

  The staff passage behind the station dates from the same work. It let staff move between the
  station, treatment and the programme rooms out of patients' sight.
* **2012:** closed after the incident at 03:17.

It stays small. The building envelope is the current ward (about 41 × 24 m with the service
passage). The one structural addition is the **staff passage** (25 × 1.75 m), behind the north
rooms. There is no atrium, no second wing and no upper floor.

## 3. Nurse encounter (Act II) — `chapter1-nurse.png`

* **Staging.**
  - **Player.** The handoff pose is in the lobby at (−4.2, 0, 0.2), facing the ledger at
    (−3.6, 1.1, −1.75).
  - **Shaw.** She enters through the **staff door (−3.0, −6.45)** in the station's back wall,
    6.5–7 m from the player: just inside her vision.
  - **The glass.** The glazed back screen means the player sees movement in the staff passage
    **through glass** before she opens the door.
  - **The tray.** The diversion tray rattles at the treatment door, about 6.5 m from her. She turns
    away from the player toward it. That is the fair response window.
* **Her round** (proposed route `nw:route:night-round`, single level, about 34 m, about 38 s at
  0.9 m/s):
  - staff passage → station (call panel, desk) → counter → lobby → hall → treatment → treatment
    staff door → staff passage → back;
  - she is visible through glass at three points: the station screen, the treatment window from
    the hall, and the open counter.
* **Player responses**, all real choices:
  1. **Retreat west** into Wing A and hide under the bed in **105** (existing
     `nw:hiding:room-105-bed-b`, door 3.5 m away).
  2. **Quiet route:** connector → maintenance → the hall's service door. Closed doors damp sound,
     and the Nurse does not use service doors (access policy, §6).
  3. **Staff locker** inside the station (existing `nw:hiding:nurses-staff`): 2 m from the desk,
     but on her route. If she hears the locker door, she inspects (Codex rule).
* **Recovery.** `review-safe-boundary` is the review desk at the station's west end. It is reached
  only after contact is broken (Codex's rule). The `checkpoint-after-nurse` anchor stands there,
  clear of the locker and the counter.
* **Lighting.** `c1.light.night-shift` → `nw:profile:nurse-encounter`: the corridor tubes dim,
  pools tighten, the electrical unease builds. The station keeps its own pool, so she stays
  readable.

## 4. Patient chase (Act IV) — `chapter1-chase.png`

**Why it is shaped like this.** The release has to happen next to the Patient's cell, because one
2011 latch system serves both the cell and the stair barrier. The stair has to be far enough away
that escaping is a journey with choices. The cell door therefore opens into the **east corridor**,
around a corner from the vestibule. The Patient steps into the junction **between** the vestibule
and the short way to the stair, and two genuinely different routes lead away from him.

* **Reveal.**
  - **Camera.** `patient-camera-start` is inside the vestibule, looking out of its door east along
    Wing B to the T-junction.
  - **The cell door.** Out of sight around the corner; you hear its latch go.
  - **His entrance.** He enters frame from the left (north) at `patient-emergence` (19.4, 0, −3.2)
    and turns to the chime.
  - **Handoff.** Back to the player inside the vestibule. He is **9.8 m** away (Codex: 8–12 m),
    and both exits (Wing B door, service door) are in view.
* **His path to the chime:** emergence → junction → Wing B → vestibule door, about 11.7 m (about
  2.9 s). After that, Codex's perception drives him: no scripted target, no warp.

| Route | Path | Length / time | What breaks his sight | The decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — the ward way** | vestibule → **west** along Wing B (gurney pinch, 1.0 m) → smoke doors (shut by the alarm: push) → hall (piano, clock) → treatment (around the exam table) → treatment staff door → staff passage east → east corridor → barrier | ~41 m, ~12 s with doors | Smoke doors at 4 m; the hall corner; treatment; the staff passage bend. A 1.25 s break loses him. | Long and well lit, away from him. You will probably lose him, but every door costs time if he is still in sight. |
| **B — the service way** | vestibule **south** door → maintenance east → maintenance-east door → east corridor north → **past his open cell** → fire door (shut by the alarm) → barrier | ~30 m, ~8.6 s | The maintenance passage. Then the junction is open for 7 m: the gamble. | Short, but it runs toward where he came from. It works if he has committed to the vestibule (listen for him through the vision panel), and fails if he is still at the junction. |
| **C — hold** | the vestibule's linen cupboard, 3 m away | ~1.5 s to enter | He cannot see into the vestibule from the junction, so the entry is unwitnessed if you go at once | He comes to the chime and searches (Codex: about 8 s, 1.8 m radius). If he does not inspect, he leaves, and route B is clear. |
| (expert) | Electrical's internal door → its corridor door (16.4) → junction | ~16 m | none | Closest to him: legal, very dangerous |

* **Obstacles** are believable furniture, not dropped debris:
  - gurneys in Wing B;
  - the exam table in treatment;
  - the piano in the hall;
  - the pipe column in the maintenance passage;
  - an abandoned trolley in the east corridor.

  The alarm's **fire doors** (hall-west, smoke pair, staff boundary, east-corridor fire door) are
  foreshadowed by "FIRE DOOR — CLOSES ON ALARM" signs from Act I. They slow both the player and the
  Patient.
* **Fair navigation.** Everything is on the ward floor; the existing grid and door navigation
  suffices. Both routes have a clear width of at least 1.0 m and hiding places along them:
  - route A: the treatment screen (new hiding spot);
  - route B: the pump-bay lockers (the migrated storage lockers).
* **Recovery after capture.** Restore `chapter1.before-patient` in the vestibule with the same
  staging, plus Codex's replay/skip choice. The Patient restores to his cell, dormant.
* **The escape.** The barrier at (19.5, −11) is latched open. The player steps onto the ward-level
  **safe landing** (`stair-safe-landing`) and **pulls the grille shut**. This is a real collision
  separation: the door closes and latches from the stair side, as the 2011 barrier was designed to
  hold people in. The Patient arrives at the bars, then the ending input lock and
  `c1.cine.ending` follow:
  - the reader (`stair-reader`): HVI-000104, RETURN ADMISSION CONFIRMED;
  - the camera rests down the stair toward the lower infirmary.

  The down flight is visible with its rail, but not entered.

## 5. Routes, shortcuts, sightlines and tension

**Routes (plan map):**
- **Act I:** 104 → Wing A → station.
- **Act III:** two ways to Electrical.
  - Through the hall and Wing B (lit, past the gallery).
  - Through the **quiet service shortcut**: connector → maintenance → Electrical's service door
    (dark, unpatrolled, closed doors).
- **Loops** that make it non-linear, all of them on one floor:
  1. lobby → hall → treatment → staff passage → station;
  2. hall → Wing B → east corridor → staff passage → treatment → hall;
  3. Wing B → vestibule → maintenance → east corridor → Wing B;
  4. lobby → connector → maintenance → hall.

| Area | Longest unbroken view | What breaks it | Tension role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wing A | 13 m (kept on purpose) | — | You can see something coming, and it can see you. Early dread. |
| Station / lobby | 7 m | Counter, the glazed screen, the staff door | Her territory; glass shows movement before contact |
| Staff passage | 9 m | Station screen, boundary fire door at x 0.2, treatment door recess | Her service route; dark and narrow |
| Ward hall | 8.6 m | Piano and clock, the alcove into the south half | Hub; three exits, so you can see her coming from one of them |
| Records | 4 m | Racks | Quiet room, one door: commitment |
| Gallery | 4 m through glass | — | Seeing the dormant Patient |
| Wing B | 9.6 m | Gurneys, the smoke doors | The reveal axis |
| East corridor | 9 m (was 18) | Alarm fire door at z −1.9, the lift lobby, the trolley | The short way out, past his cell |
| Maintenance | 10 m (was 25) | Pipe column and pump bay, door recesses | Quiet shortcut; chase route B |
