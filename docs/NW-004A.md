## 2. The Fractured Ward, at a glance

The existing ward is one 38 m corridor with rooms either side and a service loop. The plan keeps
everything that already works (Room 104, Wing A, the station, the south band, the maintenance
loop) and grows the building **north** (an atrium), **south** (a second, older patient wing) and
**up and down** (a real stair tower). Where the current long corridor becomes a hub, it is broken
up.

| Area | Where / size | Identity (why it feels different) |
|---|---|---|
| **Patient Wing A** | existing, x −20 to −7 | The known, almost-safe place: Room 104, the Blender set, the moonlit end window. A part-closed fire-door lobby and a dropped bulkhead at x −9.3 break the 38 m sightline (Option A, below). |
| **Nurses' station** | existing, x −6.8 to 0.2, z −6.4 to −1.4 | Gains a **glazed north screen and a door** onto the Observation Walk. You see the Nurse through two layers of glass before you ever meet her. |

The landmark: **a dead tree in a dry fountain** at (5, −14.5), under a cracked skylight, catching the only moonlight in the building. Strong contrast: a bright cone at the centre, black corners. ### Player route (provisional — Codex owns objectives)

Room 104 → Wing A → past the station glass (first glimpse of the Nurse) → through the station to
the Observation Walk → the atrium (landmark) → Treatment → Records 112 (the file) → Electrical 114
→ maintenance → pump junction → Wing B → east corridor → Stairwell B tower → L3 exit.

Alternatives at every stage: the gallery loop above the atrium, the Wing B / service circuit, the
existing maintenance loop. Dead ends are deliberate and few: the riser room and the Wing B sluice
each hold a hiding place.

### Nurse patrol (proposal, for Codex)

`station → Walk → atrium floor (around the tree) → Walk east door → Treatment → clinical street → station`

On every leg she is visible through glass from somewhere else:
- the station screen;
- the Walk glazing;
- the gallery above;
- the procedure room's one-way glass.

It stays on one level (y 0).

### Patient chase routes

* **The atrium circuit (new):** about 70 m. The tree and fountain are a pillar to run round, and
  the exits are lit by the `patient-chase` profile.
* **The Wing B / service circuit (new):** about 55 m.
* **The existing maintenance loop** is unchanged.

## 3. Migration risks (what could break, and the mitigation)

| Risk | Detail | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Multi-level navigation** | Codex's navigation is a grid on the floor plane. Its README says there is no floor-connection graph, autostep or navmesh yet. Enemies cannot use the gallery or the stair tower's L3/L5. | Phase 1 keeps all enemy routes on y 0. The gallery and the upper and lower landings are player-only (or scripted) until Codex adds level links. **Needs a Codex decision** for the climax chase. |
| **Room, door and patrol IDs** | Codex's NW-004 (unmerged, unseen by me) may reference current IDs and coordinates. | **Phase 1 changes no existing ID, door, collider or patrol route.** Rooms only gain doors. New IDs are listed in the JSON. Phase 2 keeps `room-107` and `storage` as IDs but changes their use. |
| **Composition counts in Codex tests** | `doorway-seams.test.ts` (collider count) and `hospital-interactions.test.ts` (hiding counts) pin exact totals. I updated them once, for 112/114. | Each phase updates them in the same commit and explains why. The hiding test then also exercises the new spots. |
| **Hiding lockers in storage** | The 4 locker hiding spots are in the storage room. Phase 2 splits it. | Keep the lockers inside the "clean utility" half (x 14.4–18), so their IDs and exits are unchanged. Otherwise migrate them with Codex. |
| **The long corridor** | A true dogleg (Option B) moves the walls, doors and waypoints of 107, 110, records and storage. | **Option A (recommended now):** break sightlines without moving the corridor centre line: a part-closed fire-door lobby at x −9.3, the existing smoke doors at x 8.4 left nearly closed, ceiling bulkheads and a hanging curtain partition. Option B only in Phase 2, if Codex wants it. |
| **Performance** | The floor area roughly triples, and the atrium is a large open volume with long views. | Per-area Blender sets loaded on approach, KTX2 textures (encoder verified: the 2048² light map drops from about 21 MB to about 2.8 MB on the GPU), shared material kit, per-area visibility groups (portal by room connections), atrium budget ≤ 25 MB GPU. |
| **World bounds and checkpoints** | `world.bounds` grows. Saves store only a checkpoint label. | Update the bounds. Checkpoints stay Codex's. |
| **Staging anchors** | Patient intro and stair ending move to the atrium and tower. | Re-stage when built. The tests re-verify every anchor. |

## 4. Proposed phasing

* **Phase 0 (safe, in progress now, needs no approval):**
  - the environmental audio library and manifest;
  - KTX2 conversion of the Room 104 set (GPU memory);
  - the shared Blender material kit;
  - lighting-profile screenshots in the real game.
* **Phase 1 (additive, needs your approval):**
  - the Observation Walk, the atrium and gallery, and the east lobby;
  - the stair tower (L3–L5);
  - Wing B and the maintenance branches;
  - Option A sightline breaks in Wing A;
  - glazing and doors added to the station and treatment room.

  No existing ID, collider or patrol route moves. Blender sets in priority order: atrium, station
  and Walk, treatment department, stair tower, then Wing B.
* **Phase 2 (needs Codex sign-off):**
  - the treatment-department enfilade (Procedure, Observation, Clean utility);
  - a corridor dogleg (Option B), if wanted;
  - multi-level enemy navigation hooks (Codex);
  - any change to patrol routes.

## 5. Interfaces for Codex (unchanged from the original NW-004A scope)

* **Lighting:** `events.emit('atmosphere:lighting', { targetId: 'nw:profile:<exploration|nurse-encounter|supernatural|patient-chase>', effect: 'restore', durationSeconds, intensity? })`.
* **Staging:** `getHospitalExtras(world).staging` (or `STAGING_ANCHORS` from `src/world`), data
  only.
* **New interactables:** `nw:inspect:records-patient-file`, `nw:inspect:electrical-breakers`.
  New hiding spot: `nw:hiding:records-cupboard`. Provisional key: `nw:key:electrical-114`.
* **No narrative triggers, objectives, puzzles or enemy behaviour** are implemented on this branch.
