## 0. The building: Halden Vale Infirmary, Ward 4 West

**Style.** An old British infirmary:
- **1930s:** Ward 4 West was built as a long Nightingale ward. That is why the corridor is so long
  and straight, why it ends at a tall window, and why the rooms are all the same width.
- **1970s:** the open ward was partitioned into single rooms (101–110). The nurses' station sits
  where the old ward sister's desk stood, open to the corridor.
- **1990s:** suspended ceilings, vinyl and fluorescent troffers. The smoke-control doors held open
  on magnets date from then.
- **Back-of-house:** what was the linen store and sluice became Medical Records (112) and the
  electrical room (114). The maintenance passage runs behind the south rooms.
- **Stairwell B:** a concrete fire stair, chained shut "until further notice".

**The size stays the same.** The existing ward is about 41 × 14 m plus its service spine and is
**large enough for Chapter 1**. A focused, interconnected floor where every room has a job is
scarier than a bigger one. The plan changes the existing building in a few precise places (§6),
each justified by a beat.

**What the existing layout already does well (and the story uses):**
- **The 38 m corridor is a weapon, not a flaw:** you can see anything coming, and it can see you.
  The chapter uses it twice: once to show the chained stair (Act I), once for the reveal (Act IV).
- **The maintenance loop** gives the chase a circuit.
- **The station** is open to the corridor, so the Nurse is a presence you watch before you meet.

## 2. Playable route per act

Speeds used: player walk 2.4 m/s, sprint 4.3 m/s. Patient pursuit 4.1 m/s. Nurse vision 7 m,
hearing ×2.4. All from Codex's profiles.

| Act | Route (beats) | Length | Time (walking, no threat) |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | 104 → corridor → (optional west: the window, the Watcher glimpse) → east past the empty station, the smoke doors, Records/Electrical doors → junction → Stair B (chained), lift (dead) → back west | ~ 60 m | 1.5–3 min with exploration |
| II | corridor near the waiting bay (beat 3, the Nurse through the treatment window) → wait for her round → station → key safe → out | ~ 15 m | 1–3 min, stealth-paced |
| III | station → corridor east → Records 112 (locked: the station key) → file → Electrical 114 → breaker | ~ 30 m | 2–3 min |

Every act ends somewhere that points at the next one: Act I ends looking back down the long
corridor toward the station's light; Act II ends with the key and the Records door in the same
corridor; Act III ends at the breaker, a step from the reveal; Act IV ends at the stair.

## 4. Nurse encounter layout (Act II)

* **Introduction through glass (G1).** From the corridor by the waiting bay, a new observation
  window into the treatment room shows the Nurse at the exam table under the dying lamp, 4–6 m away
  and behind glass. Her vision range (7 m) means she will not see you there unless you linger in
  the light. You learn what she looks like, how she moves, and that she is listening.
* **Her round (proposal for Codex's route).** The treatment table, then a new internal
  station–treatment door (G1), then the back of the station, the counter, the corridor west to room
  105, and back the same way through the treatment door. It takes about 40–60 s. The internal door
  means she enters the station from **behind**, not from the corridor you are watching. The loop
  stays on one level, so no new navigation features are needed.
* **The key safe (G2)** sits on the station's back wall: the deepest point, furthest from both
  exits. You must commit.
* **Sound is her weapon.** Broken glass by the counter (G2) is a noise trap: walking on it
  "crunches" (Codex: a noise event). Sprinting anywhere in the station is heard. Crouching past is
  safe.
* **Hiding.** The station's staff locker (existing, `nw:hiding:nurses-staff`) is 2 m from the safe.
  It is in her round, so it is risky if she sees you get in. Room 105's under-bed spot is across the
  corridor.
* **Lighting.** Codex switches to `nw:profile:nurse-encounter` when her round starts (corridor
  tubes dim, pools tighten, electrical unease), and back to `exploration` once you leave with the key.

## 5. Patient chase layout (Acts IV–V)

See `chapter1-chase.png`.

### 5.1 Reveal (beat 7)

- **Setup:** the player is in Electrical 114 at the breaker (x 17.4, z 3.2). On reset, the
  `patient-chase` profile cuts in: emergency lighting, red pulses. The alarm sounds and the smoke
  doors swing shut (x 8.4).
- **The reveal:** the player steps to the Electrical door (x 16.4). Down the corridor, 8 m west,
  the Patient's face is in the smoke doors' wired glass, under the hanging EXIT sign's glow. A short
  cinematic (anchor `nw:stage:patient-intro:*`, re-staged to the smoke doors), then control returns.
- **Why it must happen here:** the player has just made the noise; it is the one moment the
  Patient's direction is unambiguous; and the corridor's length shows exactly how little time you
  have.

### 5.2 Initial escape direction

**Away from the doors: east, or back through Electrical.** West is the Patient. The level offers
four choices within 2 seconds of the reveal. Timings below use Codex's speeds. The Patient also
loses 0.8 s pushing the smoke doors open and 0.45 s to detect.

| Option | Route | Player time | Patient arrival | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A: dash east** | junction → Stair B door (14 m) | 3.3 s | about 6.6 s | 3.3 s lead. Enough to cut the chain (proposed 1.5 s hold) **only with the cutters**. Without them it is a dead end, and the chain was shown in Act I and in the breaker note. A deliberate lesson, not a trick. |
| **B: back through Electrical** | the released service door → maintenance → pump bay (cutters) → west along the passage → connector → corridor → east to the stair | cutters at about 4 s | about 4.5 s to the service door (door shut behind you: +0.8 s) | A 1.6 s lead at the bay, then the G4 pipe column breaks line of sight, and the Patient loses you after 1.25 s unseen. The long way round is safe if you are not seen again. |
| **B2: maintenance east** | pump bay → maintenance east door → east corridor north → stair (24 m + a door) | about 6.3 s after the bay | 2–2.5 s behind | The fastest finish, cutting the chain with the Patient in view: the most cinematic, the most dangerous. |
| **C: hide** | records cupboard (6.6 m) | about 2.5 s | the Patient reaches the Electrical door at about 3.2 s | Works only if you move instantly. If it sees you enter, it inspects the cupboard and you are caught. If not, it searches for 8 s and leaves, which opens every route. |

### 5.3 Sightline breaks

| Break | Kind |
|---|---|
| The Electrical doorway (the room is out of the corridor's line) | existing |
| The east junction corner (x 18) | existing |
| The service door closed behind you | existing door, released by the breaker |
| The G4 pipe column and pump bay, offsetting the passage's 25 m straight by 0.6 m | new |
| The maintenance east door | existing |
| The station and waiting-bay recesses on the way back west | existing |

### 5.4 Doors and obstacles

- **Smoke doors:** close on the alarm and cost the Patient time.
- **Service door:** the player can shut it (it does not lock).
- **Maintenance doors:** both ends.
- **Narrow passage:** 1.4 m wide, so no passing.
- **Proposed noise traps in the passage:** pipes and a rolling bucket, Codex's call.

### 5.5 Enemy navigation

Everything is on **one level** (y 0) and inside the existing walkable grid. The routes are the
existing `nw:route:ward-loop` (corridor → east corridor → maintenance → connector), plus Records,
Electrical and the service door, which the door-aware navigation already handles. G4 only widens
the passage. **No multi-level navigation is needed:** the stairwell ending is scripted.

### 5.6 Decisions that matter

- Grab the cutters (it costs about 1 s at the bay) or keep running.
- Close the service door behind you (it buys 0.8 s) or don't stop.
- East or west from the bay (fast and visible, or slow and hidden).
- Hide or run in the first 2 seconds.

### 5.7 Recovery after capture

- **Capture:** Codex's capture cinematic, then the existing session restart.
- **Proposal:** a checkpoint **after the breaker** (beat 6). A retry starts in Electrical with the
  alarm already on, the Patient behind the smoke doors, and a **2 s re-reveal**, not the full
  cinematic.
- **Cutters:** reset on retry, so each attempt is the same puzzle. Codex's call.
- **The Patient:** should not stand within 13 m of the retry spawn.

### 5.8 Final escape and ending cinematic (beat 9)

1. **The door:** a hold-to-cut on `nw:door:stairwell-b`, then through. The door swings shut. The
   Patient strikes it and **stops**: the lore says it cannot leave the ward.
2. **Camera:** from the half landing, looking back up at the door (anchor
   `nw:stage:stairwell:landing`), the Patient's silhouette in the wired glass.
3. **G5, the stair down:** the player descends one flight to the **Ward 3 landing**. Through that
   door's glass: a corridor identical to Ward 4 West, a clock at 03:17, the tubes flickering. The
   Nurse stands at its far end. Cut to black.
4. **Alternative without G5:** the ending plays on the existing half landing, with the camera
   looking down the well into darkness.
